AI Helpdesk Ticket Router

AI project built with ChatGPT's help, submitted to my mentor for verification

An AI-powered IT helpdesk system that runs locally, with no paid cloud AI service. When a ticket is created, a local Ollama model (Qwen2.5) reads it and suggests the category, priority, summary and support team. The system then pulls troubleshooting steps from a knowledge base using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

  • Full ticket lifecycle: create, assign, route, track, resolve, close and reopen, with history and audit tracking.
  • SLA tracking by priority, including due times and breach identification.
  • Reopened tickets move up one priority level and get a fresh SLA.
  • Role-based access for admin, supervisor, operator and guest, with password hashing and sessions.
